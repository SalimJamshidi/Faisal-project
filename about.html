THE PERSON BEHIND THE PLAN

About me.

A little about my journey, my experience, and why fitness matters to me.

Hello! I'm Salim Jamshidi — a Software Engineer and Full Stack Web Developer passionate about building clean, functional digital experiences. I studied at the University of Peterborough, where I developed my technical skills and built a strong foundation in software development.

But beyond the screen, I've always been driven by discipline and determination — values forged in the ring.

Martial arts has been part of my life since I was 13 years old. I started with freestyle wrestling and MMA, eventually finding my true strength in boxing. In Afghanistan, I competed in 16 amateur MMA fights and 6 freestyle wrestling matches. At age 16, I moved to the UK and took a few years away from competition to focus on my education — but I never stopped training.

When I returned, I stepped into the ring again: 6 amateur boxing fights, followed by 4 professional bare-knuckle fights in one of the world's top bare-knuckle organisations.

Through every chapter — whether coding or training — one rule stays the same: I show up, even on my worst days. Consistency over perfection. Every single day. 🥊💻
