About me.

A little about my journey, my experience, and why fitness matters to me.

Hello! I'm Salim Jamshidi — a Software Engineer and Full Stack Web Developer passionate about building clean, meaningful digital experiences. I studied at the University of Peterborough, where I built my technical foundation and discovered my love for creating things that work well.

Beyond coding, discipline has always guided me. Martial arts and boxing have been part of my life since I was 13 — teaching me that progress doesn't come from talent alone, but from showing up, even on the hard days. That mindset carries into everything I build: consistent effort, constant learning, and always striving to do better than yesterday.

Consistency over perfection. Every single day. 🥊
