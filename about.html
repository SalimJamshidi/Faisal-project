About me.

A little about my journey, my experience, and why fitness matters to me.

My name is Faisal, and fitness has been a big part of my life for over six years. Over that time, I’ve learned that progress comes from showing up consistently, staying patient, and finding the kind of training that works for you.

I’m passionate about helping others feel stronger and more confident in their own fitness journey. Wherever you’re starting from, every step forward counts—and I’m here to encourage you to keep going.
